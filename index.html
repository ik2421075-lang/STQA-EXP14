COVID-19 Overview & Etiology

Coronavirus disease (COVID-19) is an infectious illness caused by the SARS-CoV-2 single-stranded RNA virus. It spreads primarily through respiratory droplets expelled when an infected person breathes, coughs, sneezes, or talks in close proximity.

Airborne Droplets: Droplet transmission in crowded or poorly ventilated indoor spaces.
Incubation Period: Typically 2 to 14 days, with symptom onset averaging 4 to 6 days.
Diagnostic Tests: Molecular RT-PCR and point-of-care Rapid Antigen Tests (RAT).

Symptoms & Clinical Manifestations

Most Common
  • Fever or chills (> 38°C)
  • Dry cough
  • Fatigue & weakness
  • Loss of taste or smell
Less Common
  • Sore throat & headache
  • Muscle aches & body pain
  • Gastrointestinal upset
  • Skin rash
Emergency Red Flags
  • Difficulty breathing (Dyspnea)
  • Persistent chest pressure
  • Hypoxia (SpO2 < 94%)
  • Confusion or cyanosis

Prevention & Safety Guidelines

Wear Certified Masks
Use multi-layer N95 or surgical masks in transit and hospitals.
Hand Hygiene
Wash thoroughly with soap for 20s or use >70% alcohol sanitizer.
Physical Distance
Keep 6 feet distance from symptomatic individuals.

Vaccination Information

Covishield: Recombinant viral vector. 2 doses with 84-day spacing.
Covaxin: Inactivated whole virion vaccine. 2 doses with 28-day spacing.
Corbevax: Protein subunit vaccine approved for primary & booster vaccination.
